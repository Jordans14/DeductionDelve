# AI Inhabitants

See also: [THE_DELVE_PROTOCOL](d:/DeductionDelve/docs/THE_DELVE_PROTOCOL.md), [PROTOCOL_STATES](d:/DeductionDelve/docs/PROTOCOL_STATES.md), [LEVEL_GEN](d:/DeductionDelve/docs/LEVEL_GEN.md).

## Overview
AI inhabitants are not generic enemies. They are pressure instruments within the Delve Protocol. They should shape movement, rescue, burden, and social ambiguity while preserving traversal-first identity.

## Current Live Distinction
- The live repo now has bounded host-authoritative inhabitant ecology inside the run/network owner path: ghost pressure, anomaly echoes, predator rush, and protocol watch all run through the same deterministic authority path.
- The live AI Delve under `godot/src/delve/` is not an inhabitant roster. It is a bounded intelligence ecology that shapes pre-run setup, symbolic interpretation, and public-safe carryover.
- The live runtime inhabitant set is still bounded, but it is no longer ghost-only: anomaly echoes, predator rush, and protocol watch now carry distinct signatures through that same runtime ecology path without creating a second AI owner.

## Intelligence Ecology vs Inhabitants
The live Influence Lattice and future AI inhabitants are separate layers.
- `Examiner`, `Trickster`, `Archivist`, `Cartographer`, and `Warden` are interpretive minds inside the pre-run lattice. They author tendencies through topology bias, pacing, pressure grammar, item ecology, symbolic language, and archive tone.
- Predators, Echoes, and protocol-watch behavior remain in-run pressure entities. They should consume the same deterministic owner path rather than bypass it.
- The lattice may shape conditions those entities later live inside, but it does not directly spawn or puppet inhabitant behavior at runtime.

## Predators
Predators are direct pressure entities.
- They chase, split, corner, or punish exposed movement.
- They amplify rescue pressure, burden risk, and route commitment.
- They are most useful when they complicate social interpretation rather than replace it.

## Echoes
Echoes are pattern-bearing presences.
- They repeat or distort familiar movement, sound, or route pressure.
- They reinforce the sense that the Delve remembers.
- They should deepen atmosphere, false certainty, and myth recurrence.

## Protocol Watch
Protocol watch is the bounded live expression of the Delve's regulating force.
- It intervenes when behavior, density, custody volatility, or counter-reading pressure cross certain thresholds.
- It is not a normal monster; it is the system made visible.
- Its role is to shape experimental conditions, not to turn the game into a combat-forward PvE title.
- In the live bounded implementation, protocol watch now distinguishes inspection, containment, and interdiction behavior on the existing host ecology path, including sweep traces, bounded extraction interruption, and lure-driven signal redirection.

## Design Constraints
- Sparse and pressure-first
- Branch-sensitive
- Deterministic
- Readable enough to create stories
- Never so dominant that they erase player-to-player interpretation

## Current Live Implementation Note
Live status today remains bounded and owner-safe rather than roster-heavy: ghost pressure, anomaly echoes, predator rush, and protocol-watch behavior are live, while broader inhabitant variety can still deepen later through the same authority path without creating a second runtime AI owner.
