## 1. Executive Architecture Mandate

The Delve Protocol must evolve from a materially complete but still partially convergent systemic expedition game into a **world-model-driven constitutional game** in which AI authors expedition law, not arbitrary runtime truth.

The decisive leap is **not** simply more content, more branch families, more items, or more enemies. The decisive leap is a new authorship model:

- DelveMind stops being a selector of pre-authored parts.
- DelveMind becomes a compiler of expedition law.
- Runtime becomes the lawful executor of authored possibility.
- Archive becomes the lawful interpreter of what actually happened.

The north-star mandate is fixed:

> **DelveMind must have absolute authorship over the expedition possibility space, but it must never have arbitrary runtime godmode.**

That means DelveMind may author:

- the doctrine of the expedition
- the route grammar
- the chamber family mix
- the pressure ecology
- the item ecology
- the information doctrine
- the mutation envelope
- the continuity hooks
- the bargain pools
- the curse pools
- the transformation lanes
- the identity and reward surfaces that can appear

DelveMind may **not**:

- rewrite authoritative runtime truth outside the constitution
- inject hidden outcomes not declared in authored law
- bypass host authority
- fabricate evidence after the fact
- collapse social ambiguity into opaque machine judgment

The architecture therefore separates the game into three macro layers:

1. **Authorship Layer** — DelveMind analyzes continuity, party composition, doctrine memory, world state, and quality constraints, then compiles a constitution.
2. **Runtime Law Layer** — the host-authoritative runtime executes only legal state changes defined by that constitution.
3. **Interpretation Layer** — archive, evaluation, clustering, summarization, and continuity memory interpret what happened without rewriting raw truth.

Everything in this document exists to make that separation powerful, ambitious, and unbreakable.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Design Laws

The following laws are mandatory and supersede local feature preference.

1. **Host authority is final.** No client, support model, analytics layer, modality layer, or evaluation model may become a shadow authority path.
2. **Determinism is sacred.** A seed, constitution, legal runtime inputs, and replay log must reproduce the same authoritative result.
3. **DelveMind authors law, not arbitrary truth.** It may define possibility space, reveal boundaries, and mutation permissions, but it may not bypass runtime legality.
4. **Ambiguity is allowed; arbitrariness is not.** Players may argue over meaning, but the game may not produce unreadable or unfair outcomes.
5. **All public signals must remain socially interpretable.** The game may be dense, strange, and layered, but not mechanically illegible.
6. **Continuity may bias future expeditions, but must not create permanent runaway combat power.**
7. **Persistent growth should increase interpretation, preparation, identity, and authored possibility, not permanent expedition stat dominance.**
8. **Every advanced AI output must terminate in inspectable symbolic form before it can influence runtime legality.**
9. **Every information source must have a collection, storage, use, and deletion contract.**
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** The stronger the authorship layer becomes, the stronger the XAI layer must become.
11. **Fairness and privacy are mandatory.** No modality or memory system may become a covert punishment engine.
12. **No deepfake mechanics.** No realism-based impersonation, no synthetic identity mimicry, no hidden lie-detection fiction.
13. **No opaque guilt automation.** Machine inference may support interpretation surfaces, never replace social deduction with illegible blame scoring.
14. **Every system must deepen a named loop.** Numeric perturbation without loop expansion is not sufficient depth.
15. **Readability outranks spectacle.** This is especially true for VFX, cosmetics, transformations, and build synergies.

## 3. Canonical Nomenclature and Semantic Hierarchy

Terminology drift creates schema drift, implementation drift, and player-facing confusion. The following semantic ladder is canonical.

### 3.1 Canonical Terms

- **The Delve Protocol** — the full product, world-system, and architecture.
- **Continuity** — the persistent long-horizon layer spanning memory, archive, identity, unlocks, doctrine familiarity, and world consequences.
- **Cycle** — an optional broader world-era or civilization phase. Internal until needed.
- **Expedition** — one full session from briefing to post-run archive and continuity update.
- **Delve** — the active descent operation inside an expedition.
- **Constitution** — the AI-authored master law artifact for an expedition.
- **Doctrine** — the expedition’s strategic philosophy and value system.
- **Route** — the topological graph governing traversal.
- **Chamber** — a room-scale social machine inside the route.
- **Artifact** — the custody-bearing objective object or object family whose authenticity and extraction matter.
- **Relic** — an expedition build modifier that stacks, synergizes, and alters player variables.
- **Tool** — an active execution instrument that expresses the build in moment-to-moment play.
- **Covenant** — an explicit bargain object acquired in a Vow Chamber.
- **Extraction** — the end-state crisis and resolution gate.
- **Archive** — the interpretive memory layer that stores, clusters, and re-presents expedition residue.

### 3.2 Hidden-Truth Naming Discipline

The **Cookbook path is hidden**. Its ontology must not leak into player-facing language.

The following must **not** appear in player-facing names:

- Cookbook Bargains
- Cookbook Rooms
- Cookbook Offers
- Cookbook Contracts
- Cookbook Relics

Public-safe names for that economy include:

- **Vow Chambers**
- **Covenants**
- **Witness Altars**
- **Protocol Offers**
- **Seal Chambers**

Canonical recommendation:

- **Chamber Type:** Vow Chamber
- **Bargain Object:** Covenant

### 3.3 Relationship Sentence

A player enters an **Expedition**. That expedition contains a **Delve**. The delve follows a **Constitution**. The constitution includes a **Doctrine** and a **Route**. The route is composed of **Chambers**. The expedition revolves around **Artifacts** and the systems that interpret, distort, carry, protect, or extract them. The results feed into **Continuity** through the **Archive**.

## 5. Core Lifecycle and Loop Architecture

The Delve Protocol is a stack of nested loops. Every system must explicitly deepen one or more of these loops.

### 5.1 Moment Loop

Read space → read people → move → commit → react → leave residue.

Almost every meaningful input should create one or more of:

- positional value
- custody value
- information value
- pressure value
- suspicion value
- route value

### 5.2 Chamber Loop

Enter → assess danger → understand affordances → create or interpret residue → make a route or custody decision.

A chamber is not just a room. It is a **local social machine** with topology, sightlines, secrecy affordance, witness affordance, pressure affinity, residue potential, and memory signature.

### 5.3 Route Loop

Commit → split → regroup → shortcut → reroute → mutate → expose → recover.

The route must become a **living graph loop** rather than a cosmetic room shuffle.

### 5.4 Custody Loop

Locate → inspect → verify → counterfeit → carry → transfer → seal → expose → extract.

Artifacts are the expedition spine. Every major system should be capable of bending custody play.

### 5.5 Information Loop

Observe → infer → claim → distort → corroborate → doubt → reinterpret → remember.

This is where the social deduction game truly lives.

### 5.6 Pressure Loop

Trigger → inspect → pursue → deny → distort → escalate → counterplay.

Pressure is ecosystemic, not just enemy aggression.

### 5.7 Expedition Loop

Briefing → expectation setting → doctrine presentation → delve → divergence → extraction crisis → revelation → continuity update.

### 5.8 Continuity Loop

Archive growth → doctrine familiarity growth → identity sharpening → unlock expansion → future-bias authoring.

### 5.9 Meta Loop

Players learn the world. The world learns the players. DelveMind authors more specific expeditions without becoming unfair. This is the retention spine that distinguishes a real product from a tech demo.

## 6. Persistence, Progression, and Player-Lifetime Growth

The Delve Protocol must not become a permanent-power accumulation game. Persistent growth should expand interpretation, preparation, memory, access, identity, and authored possibility.

### 6.1 Expedition-Persistent State

Resets after the expedition unless explicitly transformed into continuity residue:

- relics
- tools and tool modifications
- burdens and curses
- temporary contracts and covenants
- temporary marks and status effects
- route privileges
- ecology bargains
- local chamber memory distortions
- temporary role-surface modifications

### 6.2 Continuity-Persistent State

Persists across player lifetime:

- archive records and summaries
- doctrine familiarity and doctrine exposure history
- artifact-family knowledge
- ecology-family knowledge
- relationship memory
- codex and collection discoveries
- cosmetics and continuity identity
- continuity preparation options
- unlocks for future authored possibility

### 6.3 Semi-Persistent Continuity Traces

Bias future expeditions without creating direct static power:

- trust signatures
- betrayal signatures
- stewardship signatures
- greed signatures
- silence signatures
- coalition signatures
- archive obsession signatures
- civilization reactions
- constitution-lane likelihood biases

### 6.4 Reward Philosophy

Continuity must not feel purely punitive. It should not only mean “the system now reads you better and pressures you harder.” It must also provide positive reward through:

- identity expression
- cosmetic progression
- archive memorialization
- access to stranger authored expeditions
- continuity preparation choices
- unlock puzzles
- covenant access lanes
- doctrine-linked prestige signals

The growth fantasy is:

> “The world remembers me, I have visible history, and future expeditions are richer because of who I’ve become.”

### 6.5 Unlock Lattices

The architecture supports:

- personal unlocks
- cooperative unlocks
- competitive unlocks
- continuity unlocks
- chamber-memory unlocks
- covenant unlocks

These can span multiple expeditions so that fragments, signals, or keys found in one delve may matter later, even with different party compositions.

## 10. Role Surface Architecture

Role depth should increase through **constitution-driven surface activation**, not uncontrolled role proliferation.

### 10.1 Stable Identity vs Variable Surface

A Warden remains a Warden, but the constitution may make attestation stronger and inspection riskier in one expedition, and slower but more decisive in another.

### 10.2 Verb Families

- **Custody verbs** — inspect, seal, transfer, stabilize, counterfeit, contest
- **Topology verbs** — unlock, reroute, bridge, shutter, reveal, anchor
- **Witness verbs** — mark, expose, attest, doubt, amplify, obscure
- **Ecology verbs** — lure, pacify, redirect, provoke, ward, bait
- **Misinformation verbs** — forge, echo, duplicate, contaminate, misclassify
- **Protection verbs** — shield, carry, escort, recover, stabilize, sanctify
- **Tempo verbs** — delay, accelerate, stall, compress, escalate, de-escalate
- **Continuity verbs** — rare interactions that bind archive or long-memory consequences

### 10.3 Role Rule

No future role addition is valid unless it deepens a genuinely new loop interaction rather than merely providing another path to suspicion heat or extraction timing perturbation.

## 11. Traversal, Physics, and Baseline Mechanical Kit

The baseline physical kit is canon and expedition-wide.

### 11.1 Base Kit

Players always have:

- jump
- **double jump**
- whip
- ledge grab
- rope climbing
- object carry and throw
- bomb throw
- destructible terrain interaction

This locks in a fluid, immediately fun baseline instead of making mobility feel withheld.

### 11.2 Optional Traversal Extensions

Relics, covenants, or transformations may grant:

- glide
- dash
- grapple
- wall launch
- aerial recovery
- traversal anchoring

### 11.3 Fall Damage Policy

Fall damage exists but is **more forgiving than Spelunky**.

Recommended ladder:

- small fall → no damage
- medium fall → stability loss or short stun
- large fall → health damage
- extreme fall → health damage plus knockdown

Artifacts may increase fall consequence indirectly through carry burden or instability, but should not simply “damage you for being carried.”

### 11.4 Traversal Philosophy

Traversal must feel:

- skillful
- expressive
- crisp
- readable
- synergistic with tools and relics

## 12. Health, Stability, PvP, and Survival Model

### 12.1 Health Layers

#### Physical Health
Affected by:

- predators
- pressure attacks
- PvP attacks
- traps
- bombs
- severe falls
- harsh environmental hazards

#### Stability
Represents strain, corruption, and composure pressure.

Affected by:

- artifact burden
- relic corruption
- environmental hazards
- darkness or spore zones
- covenant costs
- curse effects
- overstrain

#### Social Trust
Not a health bar.

Influences:

- credibility
- inspection weight
- callout strength
- public consensus formation
- archive framing

### 12.2 PvP Integration

PvP is first-class and must be formally modeled.

PvP sources include:

- whip strikes
- bomb damage
- shoves and knockback
- trap triggering
- environmental weaponization
- artifact snatching violence
- chamber collapse caused by a player

PvP effects include:

- health damage
- stability damage
- stun
- knockback
- forced artifact drops
- public trace generation
- social consequence

PvP must reinforce the deduction game rather than replace it with a brawler.

### 12.3 Environmental Survival

Environmental hazards may affect health or stability through:

- spores
- cold
- heat
- unstable stone
- darkness pressure
- contamination fields
- corrosion

### 12.4 Artifact Burden Model

Artifacts should create risk amplifiers, not arbitrary direct damage. They may:

- slow carry
- increase fall consequence
- increase predator attraction
- emit witness or instability signals
- alter extraction conditions

1. **Artifacts** — custody economy

5. **Pickups / Consumables** — burst economy
6. **Covenants** — bargain economy
7. **Curses / Burdens** — negative economy
8. **Transformations** — family threshold metamorphoses

### 13.2 Ecology Rule

DelveMind does not separately sprinkle unrelated items. It compiles an ecology package containing:

- active families
- rarity bands
- synergy rules
- anti-synergy rules
- quality distributions
- visible/readability constraints
- covenant and curse pool rules
- transformation lane availability

### 13.3 Ecology Design Law

The ecology must be strong enough to create Isaac-like run identity, but must never produce screen-obscuring chaos or unreadable combat saturation.

- curse acquisition

- Witness transformation
- Echo transformation
- Burden transformation
- Forge transformation
- Hollow transformation
- Protocol transformation

Transformations alter:

- silhouette
- variable profile
- social signals
- tool behavior
- sometimes role surface or truth access

They must always remain readable.

## 20. Information Doctrine, Evidence Law, and Asymmetric Truth Access

Different expeditions must feel like different **truth environments**.

### 20.1 Information Doctrine Axes

- witness density
- forensic richness
- rumor density
- delay and reveal structure
- corruption permissions
- confidence traps
- archive contamination modes
- consensus danger

### 20.2 Evidence Categories

All evidence must be categorized as:

- **raw event truth** — deterministic factual base
- **public interpretation aid** — strong but not absolute player-facing help
- **socially arguable trace** — visible but contestable information

Archive and UI layers may label and cluster, but may not silently rewrite raw event truth.

### 20.3 Diegetic Misinformation

Valid systems include:

- counterfeit artifacts
- forged records
- false traces
- echo callouts
- corrupted witness windows
- duplicated route signatures
- chamber-memory distortion
- delayed truth release
- misclassified protocol events

### 20.4 Asymmetric Truth Access

Certain roles, relics, transformations, covenants, or chambers may grant access to different partial realities.

Examples:

- one player sees a residue class others do not
- one player receives alternate authenticity hints
- one player can read route hints hidden from others
- one transformation makes witness traces clearer but rumor traces less reliable

This must always be:

- constitution-authored
- socially debatable
- lawful and logged
- never arbitrary hallucination sludge

Asymmetric truth access is one of the deepest replayability and social-deduction multipliers in the architecture.

## 24. Monetization, Cosmetic Economy, and Readability Law

Light monetization through cosmetics is valid, but must not compromise fairness or social legibility.

- harness variants

- archive portrait treatments

### 24.2 Minor Edge Rule

Cosmetics may grant only **tiny usability-oriented edges**, generally in the ~3–5% range, and only in approved categories:

- public-trace priority law
- no screen-covering chaos law

## 36. Expanded Example Expedition Constitution

**Identity**
- Doctrine family: witness-heavy anti-clustering stewardship with ritualized custody anxiety
- Chaos band: medium-high
- Social texture: public proof is abundant but dangerous to over-trust
- Custody emphasis: authentic carry is powerful but highly visible

**Topology Grammar**
- Split/rejoin network with public witness balconies, one secret transit seam, and counterfeit-sensitive shutters
- One optional Vow Chamber may be revealed if accusation density crosses a threshold
- Extraction topology narrows into two possible lawful exits after late-phase mutation

**Pressure Grammar**
- Inspection species punishes isolated carriers
- Migration species punishes clustered groups
- Witness-corruption species attacks over-relied-on public proof channels
- Territorial species awakens only after covenant acceptance

**Item Ecology Package**
- Artifact family: witness cores with unstable attestation pulses
- Relic pools: carry, witness, route, and echo families active
- Tools: bombs, ropes, whip baseline, plus one public-signal flare tool variant
- Trinkets: witness pin, hush bead, unstable knot ring
- Pickups: dust canisters, flares, archive salts
- Covenant pool: one route privilege bargain, one witness intensification bargain
- Curse pool: Noise, Narrow Routes, Doubled Echo
- Transformations: Witness and Echo lanes active

**Information Doctrine**
- High witness density
- Medium rumor density
- Low forensic decay
- Dangerous public overconfidence
- Archive contamination allowed as interpretation distortion only

**Role Surface**
- Warden gains stronger attestation but riskier inspection exposure
- Veil gains echo-callout contamination but weaker counterfeit stability
- Steward gains better transfer stabilization but higher public consequence on failure

**Traversal and Survival Law**
- Double jump baseline preserved
- Medium fall costs stability before health
- Spore-rich chambers drain stability faster while carrying artifacts

**Custody Law**
- Authenticity may be clarified through multi-stage inspection
- Counterfeits emit false witness confidence
- Transfer windows near witness perches are publicly legible but pressure-rich

**Mutation Envelope**
- If accusation density spikes after counterfeit exposure, one witness balcony collapses, a secret route opens, and one evidence class becomes partially corrupted for the remainder of the expedition

**Continuity Hooks**
- Prior betrayal signatures raise chance of contaminated public confidence traps
- Prior archive obsession increases Vow Chamber reveal chance

**Multimodal Law**
- Opt-in voice density summaries allowed
- No CV in runtime authority
- Archive-only gesture summary allowed if all players consent

## 37. Final Commandments

1. DelveMind must author possibility space, not arbitrary runtime truth.
2. Constitution first, runtime second, archive third.
3. Host authority is final.
4. Determinism is sacred.
5. Continuity must reward identity, interpretation, and preparation, not permanent combat dominance.
6. Chambers are social machines, not generic rooms.
7. Routes are living graphs, not room shuffles.
8. Artifacts are custody economy, not build economy.
9. Relics are build economy.
10. Tools are execution economy and must plug into builds.
11. Covenants must be explicit bargains and must not leak hidden-path ontology.
12. Pressure is an ecosystem, not just enemy aggression.
13. Truth is an authored environment, not a universal constant.
14. Asymmetric truth access is allowed if lawful, debatable, and constitution-authored.
15. Mutation is powerful only when bounded by authored envelopes.
16. PvP matters, but must deepen deduction rather than replace it.
17. Cosmetics must express identity without destroying readability.
18. Readability outranks spectacle.
19. Every information channel must have a contract.
20. Every advanced model must terminate into inspectable symbolic influence.
21. Computer vision is optional, bounded, consent-driven, and never an authority path.
22. Evaluation is architecture, not afterthought.
23. Fairness, explainability, privacy, and rollback must grow with AI power.
24. Repo truth and conceptual truth must remain aligned.
