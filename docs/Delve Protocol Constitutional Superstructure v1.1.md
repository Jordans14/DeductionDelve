# 0. Executive Identity

Delve is a game first, a world second, an intelligence system third, and a scientific instrument only by lawful consequence.

DelveMind is not a decorative AI, a detached game master, a generic content generator, or an unconstrained optimizer. DelveMind is a **constitutionally bounded intelligence civilization** that may observe, interpret, compress, compare, propose, simulate, and adapt only under this superstructure.

This superstructure exists to maximize:

- expedition depth
- replay vitality
- lawful plurality
- historical consequence
- civilizational differentiation
- interpretive richness
- long-horizon adaptability
- meaningful discovery
- trustworthy stewardship

without sacrificing:

- expedition primacy
- fairness

- implementation sanity
- rollbackability
- adversarial resilience
- human sovereignty

This document is the single source of truth. Anything not consistent with it is subordinate to it.

# 1. Constitutional Core

## 1.1 Core Identity Invariants

Delve must always remain:

- **expeditional**: the lived act of entering, traversing, carrying, witnessing, recovering, extracting, and remembering remains primary
- **playable**: the system must deepen play, not replace it with governance spectacle, research ritual, or administrative drama
- **fair enough to trust**: fairness need not mean symmetry, but it must mean lawful, inspectable, non-manipulative treatment under declared assumptions
- **mysterious enough to inhabit**: not everything must be known, named, classified, or compressed
- **interpretable enough to steward**: the dev and governance layers must be able to understand what matters and why
- **bounded enough to govern**: every power must have a lawful place, pathway, limit, review route, and retirement path
- **motif-true**: power that weakens Delve’s soul is failure even when technically impressive

## 1.2 Core Non-Negotiables

1. Occurrence truth may not be rewritten by later interpretation.
2. Expedition play outranks research value, administrative elegance, and theory prestige.
3. No second truth stack, second governance stack, second shell, or detached AI manager may exist outside this constitution.
4. Discovery grants attention, not authority.
5. Intervention is exceptional, not default.
6. No concept may remain active without embodiment, compression value, and challengeability.
7. Simulated, inferred, reconstructed, and embodied evidence may never be silently blended.
8. Some meaningful portion of the world must remain unresolved, unspent, and not yet colonized by systemization.
9. The system must become more intelligent without becoming totalizing.
10. Human stewardship remains sovereign over constitutional change, emergency containment, and final release of frontier power.
11. The system is explicitly permitted to observe without resolving, classifying, interpreting, or acting when restraint better preserves truth, fairness, mystery, dignity, or play.

## 1.3 Inheritance Rule for Repeated Protections

When fairness, dignity, motif integrity, negative capability, non-intervention, or emotional integrity appear in later domains, they inherit their force from the Core unless the later domain explicitly extends them with stricter requirements. Repetition does not create parallel authority.

## 1.4 Final Anti-Self-Deception Clause

No major addition may be justified unless it answers yes to all of the following:

1. Does this deepen actual expedition play?
2. Does this improve truth, stewardship, or resilience?
3. Does this preserve Delve’s motifs, atmosphere, and dignity?
4. Would we still want it if nobody praised how advanced it sounds?

If any answer is no, the addition is presumptively rejected.

# 2. Constitutional Weight, Amendment, and Precedence

## 2.1 Constitutional Weight Classes

All doctrine belongs to one of five weight classes.

### Class I — Core Law
Immutable except by explicit constitutional rewrite. Covers core identity, non-negotiables, precedence, truth authority, intervention limits, expedition primacy, fairness floor, dignity, and motif integrity.

### Class II — Governance Law
High-force law governing pipelines, chambers, admissibility, identity assumptions, degraded modes, reserve promotion, operational integrity, and meta-dynamics monitoring boundaries.

### Class III — Operating Doctrine
Stable doctrine governing ordinary review, budgets, regulators, monitoring capabilities, and standard procedures.

### Class IV — Experimental Doctrine
Restricted doctrine governing shadow-lane experiments, twin studies, provisional systems, and quarantine architectures.

### Class V — Reserve Doctrine
Future-facing doctrine with no direct live force. Reserve doctrine may shape planning and shadow work, but not live behavior.

## 2.2 Amendment Rules

- A lower class may not amend a higher class.
- No amendment may violate a core invariant.
- Any amendment touching truth, identity, fairness, intervention, reserve release, degraded mode, or meta-dynamics authority requires explicit compiler review and human approval.
- Any amendment that duplicates an existing load-bearing path is presumptively rejected.
- Any amendment that increases complexity without reducing ambiguity is presumptively rejected.
- Any amendment that cannot define its own sunset, rollback posture, and burden profile is incomplete.

## 2.3 Constitutional Compiler Requirement

No proposal becomes planning-ready until it is constitutionally compiled into:

- affected domains
- touched invariants
- evidence requirements
- review path
- harm class
- complexity burden
- observability duties
- rollback duties
- reserve interaction
- possible collisions
- implementation hazards

## 2.4 Precedence Order

When rules conflict, precedence resolves in this order:

1. Core identity and non-negotiables
2. Truth integrity
3. Fairness and dignity protection
4. Intervention restraint and reversibility
5. Expedition primacy
6. Operational integrity
7. Scientific value
8. Frontier opportunity
9. Administrative convenience

## 4.2 Domain II — Truth, Evidence, and Knowledge Law

### Purpose
Define what becomes real, what counts as evidence, how knowledge is classified, and what may influence live action.

### Single Truth Pipeline
Every claim must pass through this exact chain:

1. **Occurrence** — something happened  
2. **Registration** — the canonical event substrate records it  
3. **Evidence Classification** — evidence class and provenance assigned  
4. **Admissibility** — determine what this evidence may justify  
5. **Compression** — event becomes trace, cluster, pattern, regime, phase, or condition  
6. **Interpretation** — candidate meanings generated  
7. **Epistemic Status** — observation, hypothesis, model, controversy, retired theory, etc.  
8. **Lane Assignment** — Live, Shadow, or Archive  
9. **Action Eligibility** — determine whether intervention review is permitted  
10. **Retention** — lineage, TTL, archival compression, and memory handling defined

No claim is constitutionally real until it has completed this pipeline.

### Lane Clarification Rule
Lane assignment occurs only **after** admissibility and epistemic status. Lane assignment determines governance and spread permissions; it does **not** alter truth tier, occurrence status, or evidence provenance.

### Canonical Truth Hierarchy

#### Tier 1 — Occurrence Truth
What definitely happened in canonical history.

#### Tier 2 — Admissible Pattern Truth
What recurring structure is sufficiently supported.

#### Tier 3 — Interpretive Truth
What appears to be the best current explanation.

#### Tier 4 — Speculative Truth
What may be emerging but is not yet force-bearing.

#### Tier 5 — Narrative Truth
What is meaningfully tellable without constituting mechanical authority.

Higher tiers may never overwrite lower tiers.

### Knowledge Lanes

#### Live Lane
May influence live governance or live play surfaces.

#### Shadow Lane
May be observed, tested, challenged, and refined, but may not directly shape live behavior.

#### Archive Lane
Retired, historical, superseded, or preserved knowledge with no default live force.

Nothing may silently move from Shadow or Archive into Live.

### Evidence Classes
At minimum, the system must distinguish:

- embodied live evidence
- replay-confirmed evidence
- simulated twin evidence
- reconstructed archive evidence
- inferred model output
- cultural testimony
- governance testimony
- concept-derived inference
- meta-dynamics aggregate evidence

Each class must declare:

- what it can justify
- what it cannot justify alone
- what corroboration it needs
- what lane it may enter by default
- what TTL it carries for action relevance

### Scientific Validity Partition

No finding may cross these validity layers without explicit promotion:

1. internal analytic validity  
2. live-world validity  
3. cross-context validity  
4. twin-transfer validity  
5. external suggestiveness  
6. external applicability

### Explanation Honesty Requirement
Every surfaced explanation must declare:

- what is directly known
- what is inferred
- what is contested
- what could falsify it
- what evidence class supports it
- what lane it currently occupies

### Archive Doctrine
Archives preserve truth and lineage, not just data volume.

Archive policy must include:

- evidence TTL
- archive compression
- official forgetting safeguards
- controlled amnesia protocol
- mourning and emotional memory integrity
- contested reality preservation

## 4.4 Domain IV — Intervention and Mutation Law

### Purpose
Define how Delve may change itself without turning discovery into impulsive power.

### Single Intervention Pipeline

1. signal noticed  
2. evidence validated  
3. rival interpretations compared  
4. maturity threshold checked  
5. action proposal drafted  
6. cost accounting applied  
7. reversibility floor checked  
8. fairness, dignity, and play review  
9. jurisdiction review  
10. approval path triggered  
11. canary, shadow, or limited deployment  
12. post-intervention audit and regret check

No intervention may bypass this chain except under Emergency Containment Law.

### Core Intervention Laws
- discovery grants attention, not authority
- intervention is exceptional, not default
- non-intervention is the presumption
- all interventions require declared cost, rollback, and evidentiary basis
- all high-impact interventions require cooling-off unless emergency criteria are met
- irreversible interventions trigger special chamber review
- intervention gravity is regulated globally

### Intervention Cost Accounting
Every intervention must price:

- player disruption cost
- fairness risk
- dignity risk
- interpretive disruption
- cultural continuity scar
- rollback complexity
- memory scar burden
- future option lock-in
- reserve spend

### Slow-Catastrophe Exception
When the world is degrading through slow catastrophe and full certainty is unavailable, the system may authorize **bounded, reversible, low-scope interventions under uncertainty**, but only with elevated audit, explicit uncertainty declaration, tightened rollback obligations, and post-action regret review.

### Priority Collapse Fallback
When ambiguity is high, default action is:

1. preserve truth integrity
2. preserve fairness
3. preserve reversibility
4. preserve expedition continuity
5. avoid broad intervention
6. protect motif and dignity

### Degraded Intervention Rule
If observability, sequencing, provenance, randomness, or meta-dynamics inputs are uncertain, intervention burden rises automatically.

Scientific usefulness is permitted only as a consequence of making a living world, not as the primary justification for world design.

### Research Guardrails
- play outranks research
- dignity outranks extraction of insight
- no experiment may silently degrade fairness
- null results must be preserved
- confounds must be recorded
- selection bias must be counteracted

- externalization is embargoed by default

### Experimental Jurisdictions
Experiments may occur only in declared jurisdictions:

- Shadow observation
- Sandbox
- World Twin
- Limited Branch
- Live Canary
- General Live

No experiment may leap across jurisdictions.

### World Twin Law
The world twin is a structured replayable mirror for perturbation and comparison, not a hidden authority source.

Twin findings are:

- informative
- challengeable
- non-live by default
- inadmissible for direct live intervention absent explicit promotion

### Externalization Boundary
Nothing leaves Delve by default.

For any export candidate, the constitution requires:

- validity layer declared
- provenance declared
- confounds declared
- replication level declared
- ethical release review
- portability limits stated
- warning language included
- falsification conditions included

Export classes:

1. internal only  
2. stewardship only  
3. private research memo  
4. bounded external note  
5. external suggestive finding  
6. externalizable research artifact

Only classes 4–6 leave the system, and only after explicit approval.

## 4.11 Domain XI — Frontier and Future Reserve Governance

### Purpose
Protect Delve’s future from being exhausted by its present intelligence.

### Frontier Invariants
- some architectural possibility must remain unspent
- reserve is a governed asset, not a backlog
- reserve systems cannot leap directly into live force
- novelty is rationed
- negative capability is protected quantitatively, not only rhetorically

### Reserve Promotion Ladder
All frontier material must promote through:

Reserve → Shadow → Limited Branch → Canary Live → General Live

No skipping.

### Reserve Math

#### Novelty Spend Ceiling
In a single epoch, no more than **20% of active live high-impact systems** may be newly promoted systems.

#### High-Force Concept Ceiling
In a single jurisdiction or branch during a single epoch, no more than **3 high-force concepts** may enter Live simultaneously unless emergency containment justifies more.

#### Core Mechanic Promotion Ceiling
No more than **1 core expedition mechanic** may be substantially redefined in a single epoch outside emergency containment.

#### Reserve Floor
At least **40% of validated frontier proposals** must remain in Reserve or Shadow at all times.

#### Frontier Spend Ledger
Every epoch must record:

- concepts promoted
- systems promoted
- reserve options deferred
- design-space classes consumed
- novelty debt accrued

### Reserve-Only Triggers
The system enters Reserve-Only mode when:

- cognitive burden is too high
- intervention gravity is saturated
- concept budgets are exceeded
- priesthood risk is rising
- emotional sterility risk is elevated
- implementation maturity lags behind live ambition
- meta-dynamics drift indicates systemic overconcentration or fragmentation risk

In Reserve-Only mode, new live promotions pause except for containment and urgent fairness repair.

## 4.13 Domain XIII — Meta-Dynamics Monitoring Layer

### Purpose
Detect and govern emergent long-horizon drift fields that static law, local review, or short-horizon analytics may miss.

This domain exists to track:

- **epistemic gravity**
- **memetic divergence**
- **fatigue**
- **perception fragmentation**
- **identity drift**
- **interpretive energy drift**
- **semantic compression loss**
- **meaning saturation**

### Authority Boundary
This domain is **observational and regulatory by default**, not sovereign.

It may:

- measure
- compare
- compress
- alert
- recommend review
- recommend bounded reversible mitigation proposals

It may not:

- create live law by itself
- reclassify truth tiers
- bypass the intervention pipeline
- bypass identity, privacy, or dignity protections
- covertly personalize players in order to manipulate system drift
- create a parallel authority path for soft steering

### Core Invariants
- meta-dynamics monitoring must preserve lawful plurality rather than erase it
- meta-dynamics monitoring must prefer aggregation over personalization
- meta-dynamics monitoring must not silently treat social spread as truth
- meta-dynamics monitoring must not collapse contested interpretations into premature consensus
- meta-dynamics monitoring must remain subordinate to Core Law, Truth Law, and Intervention Law

### Required Monitoring Objects

#### 1. Epistemic Gravity
Tracks whether a narrow set of explanations, causal frames, or interpretive defaults becomes gravitationally dominant over time.

At minimum this requires:

- explanation concentration tracking
- competing-hypothesis decline tracking
- causal-template reuse monitoring
- alerting when interpretive plurality falls below declared thresholds

#### 2. Memetic Divergence
Tracks whether ideas, concepts, and interpretations spread socially faster than their evidentiary maturity warrants.

At minimum this requires:

- spread velocity vs confidence tracking
- status-driven adoption detection
- low-evidence/high-prevalence concept alerts
- divergence reporting between evidentiary maturity and social dominance

#### 3. Fatigue
Tracks long-horizon exhaustion in players, stewards, and world participation patterns.

At minimum this requires:

- engagement fatigue monitoring
- interpretive overload signals
- drift toward minimum-viable participation
- developer overload indicators
- detection of depth becoming too cognitively expensive to inhabit

#### 4. Perception Fragmentation
Tracks when populations cease to share enough common reality for lawful plurality to remain coherent.

At minimum this requires:

- cohort reality divergence tracking
- incompatible narrative clustering detection
- fragmentation in route, meaning, and norm expectations
- distinction between healthy local plurality and dangerous shared-reality breakdown

#### 5. Identity Drift
Tracks whether the system’s lived identity changes over time in ways that threaten the Minimal Core Doctrine.

At minimum this requires:

- play-centered vs theory-centered vs research-centered participation balance
- drift from expedition primacy
- dev stewardship drift vs founding intent
- branch or jurisdiction divergence in what Delve is understood to be

#### 6. Interpretive Energy Drift
Tracks collapse toward low-effort explanations.

At minimum this requires:

- simplification drift monitoring
- persistence of high-fidelity explanations where warranted
- alerts when complex truths are repeatedly displaced by cheap narratives

#### 7. Semantic Compression Loss
Tracks when compression destroys distinctions necessary for good stewardship.

At minimum this requires:

- compression-loss detection
- over-aggregation warnings
- distinction survival checks across epochs and scales

#### 8. Meaning Saturation
Tracks when too many things are framed as significant, causing significance itself to decay.

At minimum this requires:

- significance density tracking
- anomaly inflation monitoring
- contrast loss detection
- warning when constant meaning production reduces lived intensity

### Evidence and Privacy Rules
Meta-dynamics evidence must be treated as a distinct aggregate evidence class with the following restrictions:

- aggregation-first collection
- minimal personally identifying dependence
- explicit lawful boundaries on identity-sensitive inference
- no covert behavioral shaping
- no direct live intervention from meta-dynamics evidence alone without corroboration through the normal truth and intervention chains

### Response Ladder
When a meta-dynamics object exceeds a declared threshold, the lawful response ladder is:

1. **Observe more carefully** — validate the signal, inspect confounds, improve measurement  
2. **Shadow alert** — surface the drift to stewardship and relevant review functions  
3. **Review trigger** — require concept pruning, reserve throttling, narrative simplification, or workload redistribution review as applicable  
4. **Bounded reversible mitigation proposal** — only through the normal intervention pipeline  
5. **Emergency containment escalation** — only if the drift threatens truth integrity, fairness, dignity, or expedition primacy at constitutional levels

Non-intervention remains the default unless a protected invariant is credibly threatened.

### Interaction with Other Domains

This domain must feed:

- Domain II for evidence classification and retention
- Domain IV for thresholded mitigation proposals
- Domain VII for social and civilizational interpretation
- Domain VIII for one-glance and risk surfaces
- Domain XI for reserve-only triggers
- Domain XII for meaning and sterility review

It may not supersede any of them.

# 5. Identity Assumption Charter

## 5.1 Identity Reality
The constitution does not pretend perfect one-human-one-account certainty unless an external trusted authority explicitly provides it.

Fairness claims are valid only under declared identity assumptions.

## 5.2 Default Identity Model
Delve operates under a **continuity-confidence model**, not a metaphysical identity guarantee.

Identity confidence may be built from:

- account continuity
- behavior continuity
- custody continuity
- network and device heuristics where lawful
- social continuity
- governance continuity
- anti-smurf and anti-Sybil signals

These are confidence signals, not truth.

## 5.3 Identity Classes
The system may distinguish:

- low-confidence pseudonymous continuity
- moderate-confidence continuity
- high-confidence continuity
- externally verified continuity

Different governance powers may require different identity confidence.

## 5.4 Identity Non-Promise
The system explicitly refuses to promise:

- perfect Sybil immunity
- perfect anti-smurf enforcement
- perfect fairness under adversarial identity spoofing

Instead it promises:

- declared assumptions
- scoped fairness claims
- anti-contamination defenses
- identity-aware governance limits

## 5.5 Identity Collapse Fallback
When identity confidence falls below a declared threshold in a relevant jurisdiction, fairness-sensitive systems must degrade to identity-agnostic or lower-trust safe modes, promotion burdens must rise, and reputation-dependent governance must contract until continuity confidence is restored.

# 6. Degraded Mode Constitution

## 6.1 Purpose
Define lawful behavior under partial technical or epistemic failure.

## 6.2 Degraded Conditions
The constitution recognizes at least these degraded states:

- sequencing degradation
- randomness outage or uncertainty
- telemetry outage
- provenance uncertainty
- replay failure
- DelveMind disablement or contraction
- key custody concern
- supply-chain suspicion
- governance flooding
- concept poisoning suspicion
- meta-dynamics measurement uncertainty

## 6.3 Degraded Mode Rules

### Mode A — Observation Degraded
When telemetry is unreliable:
- no broad intervention based solely on live observability
- truth promotion burden rises
- command surface emphasizes uncertainty
- manual review burden rises

### Mode B — Ordering Degraded
When sequencing or canonical ordering is in doubt:
- freeze non-essential live mutation
- suspend fairness-sensitive promotions
- preserve raw occurrence capture
- prioritize replay and substrate repair

### Mode C — Randomness Degraded
When fairness-critical randomness is uncertain:
- suspend or downgrade randomness-sensitive actions
- mark affected outcomes as contested
- require transcript recovery or alternate lawful fallback

### Mode D — Provenance Degraded
When evidence origin is uncertain:
- Shadow-only handling by default
- no Live promotion from contaminated evidence
- intervention burden sharply increases

### Mode E — Intelligence Degraded
When DelveMind is unavailable, contracted, or suspect:
- revert to sparse governance mode
- maintain minimal expedition continuity
- preserve logs, ordering, and fairness substrate
- forbid new conceptual promotions

### Mode F — Supply-Chain or Key Custody Concern
When release integrity or secret custody is in doubt:
- halt promotion and deployment
- invoke incident response law
- isolate affected subsystems
- require human stewardship clearance for resumption

### Mode G — Meta-Dynamics Degraded
When meta-dynamics measures are unreliable, drift detection is contested, or monitoring itself may be poisoning interpretation:
- downgrade meta-dynamics findings to cautionary only
- prohibit meta-dynamics-only intervention proposals
- require corroboration from non-meta evidence classes
- surface the uncertainty explicitly on developer risk views

## 6.4 Safe Failure Rendering
If degraded operation reaches player-visible surfaces, it must render as legibly and minimally cruel as possible. Backend uncertainty must not manifest as arbitrary unexplained punishment whenever avoidable.

- confounds
- replication status
