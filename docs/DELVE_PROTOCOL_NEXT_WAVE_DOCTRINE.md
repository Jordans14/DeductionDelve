## 0. Purpose of this document

This document is the fully written doctrine form of the finalized next-wave ledger.

It exists to do five things at once:

1. Preserve the repo-grounded foundations that already exist.
2. Define the exact next-wave expansion domains that should be built.
3. Prevent duplication, parallel architecture, and doctrine drift.
4. Encode fairness, readability, creativity, and long-horizon resilience as hard constraints.
5. Give Codex a complete, coherent basis for producing a full implementation plan.

This is **not** a brainstorming memo.  
This is **not** a feature wishlist.  
This is **not** permission to bolt on adjacent systems.

This is the canonical planning doctrine for the next wave.

- burden
- custody
- interpretation
- recurrence

- trust under pressure

Every future system must remain recognizably in service of those thematic primitives.

## 4. Global master doctrines

These doctrines apply everywhere.

### 4.1 Build-on-existing doctrine
- Extend live owners before inventing new top-level owners.
- Prefer taxonomy extension over subsystem sprawl.
- Do not recreate already-established foundations under new names.
- All abstraction must route back into real play consequences.

### 4.2 Shared-reality doctrine
- Shared world truth remains primary.
- No broad per-player fabricated realities.
- Bounded private overlays are allowed only when they do not contradict shared state.
- Asymmetry should come from lawful role/state/item access, not secret fake worlds.

### 4.3 No-spite doctrine
- The system may evolve conditions.
- It may not become a personalized weakness-hunting engine.
- Adaptation must remain ecological/systemic rather than vindictive.
- Recovery, suppression, remission, and counterplay must remain real.

### 4.4 Fairness and dignity doctrine
- Fairness must be both real and perceptible.
- Players must be allowed to fail intelligently.
- The system must not feel like it is cheating.
- Losses must preserve agency, meaning, and learning.
- Success must feel earned, not arbitrarily bestowed.

### 4.5 Signal hierarchy doctrine
At all times, signals must resolve into a priority stack:
1. survival clarity
2. custody/extraction state
3. immediate threat
4. encounter topology
5. social signals
6. economy/pathology background pressure
7. memory/interpretation emphasis

Nothing lower may drown out anything higher.

### 4.6 Negative-space doctrine
- Not every room, run, or branch should be maximally dense.
- Quiet, absence, dormancy, and withheld mystery are necessary design materials.
- Contrast is part of coherence.

### 4.7 Proof-through-play doctrine
- No high-level concept may exist without direct play expression.
- Every doctrine claim must produce observable consequences.
- Abstract sophistication is not enough.

### 4.8 Content-worthiness doctrine
Before adding any content, ask:
- what pressure does it create?
- what choice does it force?
- what story does it enable?
- what identity does it reinforce?
- why does it deserve to exist?

### 4.9 Cross-scale coherence doctrine
Every major addition must be checked at:
- moment scale
- encounter scale
- run scale
- crawl scale
- world-memory scale
- player continuity scale

### 4.10 Identity anchoring doctrine
All evolution must remain recognizably about:
- burden
- custody
- interpretation
- recurrence
- intervention
- memory
- trust under pressure

### 4.11 Resolution-diversity doctrine
Major situations must admit multiple valid resolution verbs, including:
- endure
- escape
- carry through
- stabilize
- suppress
- redirect
- reclaim
- expose
- sacrifice
- extract

### 4.12 Cognitive-budget doctrine
Each run has a maximum effective cognitive load.
Complexity must read as depth, not confusion.

## 5. Explanation and perceptible meaning

The game already evolves. The next wave must ensure players can meaningfully understand that evolution.

### 5.1 Explanation packet pipeline
All major evolution-facing surfaces should be expressible through bounded explanation packets containing:
- trigger
- escalation
- consequence
- interpretation

### 5.2 Explanation layers
The system should explain itself through three layers:

#### Immediate layer
Moment-to-moment readable signals:
- telegraphs
- pressure shifts
- objective/custody changes
- active recovery windows

#### Run layer
Compact causal framing:
- why this encounter formed
- why this pressure intensified
- what the run is asking of the players

#### Post-run/meta layer
Compressed reflective explanation:
- what changed
- what mattered
- how the world now interprets the result

### 5.3 Mystery preservation
Players do not need total truth.
They need enough lawful signal to form strong mental models.
Explanation should be bounded, confidence-aware, and non-saturating.

### 5.4 Self-interpretation doctrine
The system should not only evolve.
It should interpret its own evolution.

## 9. Cosmetics, continuity, and monetization doctrine

### 9.1 Cosmetic structure
Cosmetics split into:
1. expression layer
2. modulation layer
3. identity continuity layer

### 9.2 Continuity-bearing identity artifacts
Cosmetics are not just vanity. They are one of the most persistent continuity surfaces in the entire game.

### 9.3 Gameplay modulation
Cosmetics may affect play, but only under a doctrine-safe sidegrade budget.
They should bias playstyle more than increase raw ceilings.

### 9.4 Monetization stance
There must be:
- earnable cosmetics
- optional adjacent paid acquisition
- no hard pay-to-win
- no randomized exploitative monetization
- no unfair informational/readability advantage

### 9.5 Monetization fairness contract
Paid cosmetics may not:
- dominate outcomes
- bypass systems
- create exclusive hard-power states
- exceed the fairness envelope of earnable equivalents
- undermine player trust

### 9.6 Normalization mode
The system should support normalization for competitive fairness-sensitive contexts if needed:
- disable or normalize cosmetic mechanical deltas in certain ranked/testing modes
- preserve visual identity while flattening edge values

### 9.7 Cosmetic climates
Cosmetic prestige/fashion climates should evolve culturally through the same world-memory/continuity logic as other systems.

- boss configuration

## 20. Final systemic resilience hardening

### 20.1 Perceived fairness doctrine
The system must signal fairness, not only possess it.

### 20.2 Anti-optimization doctrine
Preserve productive imperfection.
Do not converge into sterile perfection.

### 20.3 Exploit absorption doctrine
Absorb and reinterpret exploits when possible instead of merely nerfing them.

### 20.4 System resurrection priority
Forgotten or cooled systems should remain eligible for meaningful return.

### 20.5 System priority stack
At runtime, the game must know what matters most.

### 20.6 System interference doctrine
Allow:
- modulation
- amplification
- suppression
- reinterpretation

Disallow:
- silent contradiction
- invisible overrides
- incoherent cascading collapse

### 20.7 Learning-velocity doctrine
Players should be able to learn gradually. Mutation should not outrun reinforcement.

### 20.8 Memory-decay doctrine
Not everything persists cleanly forever.
Support:
- fading
- distortion
- mythification
- institutional rewriting

### 20.9 Failure-transformation doctrine
Failure should change future conditions, not merely terminate play.

### 20.10 Meta-collapse prevention
Use dominance detection, strain markers, reinterpretation, and soft counter-pressure to keep long-term metas alive.

### 20.11 Player creative contribution
Players should be able to influence future system evolution through what they do repeatedly enough.

## 21. Telemetry, evaluation, and governance safety

The next wave requires stronger measurement and containment so ambition does not outrun stability.

### 21.1 Telemetry-to-evaluation pipeline
Codex planning should assume a telemetry path that captures:
- run identity
- active regimes
- active lifecycle states
- encounter and apex composition
- explanation packet outputs
- cognitive load indicators
- fairness/dignity triggers
- monetization normalization mode
- dominant-strategy strain signals
- experiment outcomes

### 21.2 Deterministic replay bundles
For every significant run, the architecture should support:
- generation seed
- active constitution/control surfaces
- equipped cosmetic modulation loadout
- experiment set
- active regimes
- apex placements
- world-memory snapshot hash
- explanation packet outputs

These form forensic run packages for debugging, fairness review, and experiment governance.

### 21.3 Measurement integrity
Telemetry must not be distorted by:
- hidden client-only state
- cosmetics-only presentation differences
- non-deterministic explanation generation
- ambiguous mode normalization

### 21.4 Governance safety valves
The architecture should support:
- quarantine of unstable experiments
- rollback of harmful novelty
- suppression of exploit-dominant surfaces
- fairness veto enforcement
- dignity veto enforcement
- saturation throttles
- mode-based normalization
