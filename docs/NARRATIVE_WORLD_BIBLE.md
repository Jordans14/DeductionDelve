## Narrative Philosophy
The story of The Delve Protocol is produced first by physical play. Narrative systems observe, compress, remember, and reinterpret what runs, crawls, and cultures do over time. The world should feel layered, contested, and historically alive without collapsing ambiguity too early.

## The Delve Protocol
Publicly, the Delve Protocol is the name of the expedition program governing descent into the labyrinth. Internally, it is the name of a much larger system whose deeper purpose is hidden from ordinary participants.

## The Expedition Program
Expeditions are organized, broadcast, analyzed, and culturally discussed. Delvers understand themselves as participants in a dangerous public descent system with risk, prestige, scandal, and long-form crawl identity.

## The Labyrinth
The labyrinth is a world-spanning constructed environment with branch ideologies, pressure climates, and symbolically loaded places. It should feel older, deeper, and more observant than the official story fully admits.

## Artifacts
Artifacts are not just loot. They are burdens, evidence, temptations, prestige objects, cursed invitations, and social pressure points. Their meaning changes with branch climate, crawl state, and cultural memory.

## Branch Cultures
Branches accumulate public reputations, feared reputations, rescue associations, challenge associations, confrontation associations, and symbolic echoes. Their cultural meaning can layer, cool, revive, and invert over time.

## Broadcast Culture
The world watches crawls as public stories. Broadcast culture frames, judges, compresses, exaggerates, and remembers. It should feel serious, eerie, and culturally charged rather than comedic or streamer-styled.

## The Archive
The Archive preserves and interprets patterns. It is the official cultural memory surface and the place where repeated stories, branch echoes, item lineages, rivalries, and crawl identities are compared.

## The Cookbook Myth
The Delve Anarchist's Cookbook exists at the edge of accepted knowledge. It is not a public rulebook but a dangerous cultural rumor about players who discovered how to break the pressures that define the Delve.

## Truth Layers

### LAYER 1 - The Expedition Program (Public Truth)
The public world says the Delve Protocol is a dangerous expedition system built around descent, extraction, artifacts, branch knowledge, and public survival culture.

### LAYER 2 - The Planetary Experiment (Core Reveal)
The deeper truth is that the expedition program is part of a planetary-scale experiment in pressure, behavior, coordination, deception, sacrifice, and adaptation.

### LAYER 3 - The Learning Delve (Post-Core)
Past the core reveal, the Delve is understood as a system that has begun to learn from repeated descents, myth formation, branch recurrence, and cultural memory.

### LAYER 4 - The Meta Layer
At the outermost edge, the Delve suggests a still-larger frame beyond the expedition, archive, and learning labyrinth itself.

## Cookbook and the Truth Layers
Cookbook fragments should emerge through Layer 3 anomalies. They are signs that some players have begun discovering methods for stepping outside normal Protocol expectations without being given direct explanatory lore.
