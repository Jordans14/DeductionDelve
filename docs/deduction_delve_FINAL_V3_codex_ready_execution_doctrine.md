# 1. DOCTRINE STATUS AND ROLE

This document is the final authoritative doctrine for the full governed evolving reality architecture of Deduction Delve and the execution scaffolding Codex must use to plan and implement it.

This doctrine defines, without compression and without inference gaps:

- what Deduction Delve is
- what DelveMind is
- what may evolve
- what may never be erased
- how theories, observables, procedures, factions, rituals, archives, cognitive fields, maps, and forbidden methods coexist
- how the world expands without replacing its baseline
- how Codex must sequence implementation
- how abstract systems must be concretized
- how to avoid parallel architecture
- how to preserve unlimited design while still enabling disciplined execution

This doctrine supersedes all compressed summaries.

Nothing in this doctrine is optional flavor.
Everything here is architecture, implementation, governance, or review law.

# 2. NON-NEGOTIABLE IDENTITY OF THE GAME

Deduction Delve is:

> a deterministic, host-authoritative, artifact-centered expedition game embedded inside a cumulative, governed, recursively self-improving civilization-scale experimental reality.

It is not:
- a runtime AI improvisation sandbox
- a director that secretly cheats to stay interesting
- a lore-heavy abstraction detached from play
- a content churn machine
- a system that discards past meaning to create novelty
- a flat roguelike with cosmetic continuity

It is:
- a lived descent game
- a burden, route, rescue, witness, custody, and extraction game
- a civilization of interpretation
- an ecology of fields, factions, archives, theories, scars, rituals, maps, and forbidden methods
- a recursive research system that studies humanity through bounded world design
- a world that remembers without becoming unreadable
- a world that evolves without losing its baseline

# 3. ABSOLUTE LAWS

The following laws are mandatory.

## 3.1 Artifact Centrality Law
Artifacts remain central to:
- motive
- route
- custody
- burden
- extraction
- suspicion
- legitimacy
- archive conflict
- faction competition
- ritual significance
- cultural dispute
- theory formation

No subsystem may reduce artifacts to background dressing.

## 3.2 Runtime Legality Law
Once a run begins, runtime truth is locked by the pre-run deterministic generation contract.

No live runtime system may secretly rewrite:
- artifact truth
- chamber truth
- hazard truth
- evidence truth
- role truth
- map truth
- route truth
- odds of success
- difficulty in response to current player performance

## 3.3 Host Authority Law
All runtime truth is host-authoritative.

## 3.4 No Runtime Cheating Law
DelveMind, Archive, Factions, Cognitive Fields, and all governance layers are forbidden from mid-run cheating.

They may author future possibility space.
They may not secretly rewrite live truth.

## 3.5 Sacred Baseline Protection
There exists a protected baseline stack that may be expanded but never erased.

The sacred baseline includes:
- sanctioned expedition legibility
- ordinary descent sufficiency
- artifact centrality
- burden/rescue/witness/route grammar
- foundational archive anchors
- core map literacy
- recoverable skill expression
- protected cultural continuity surfaces

Expansion is lawful.
Replacement of the baseline is not.

## 3.6 Cumulative Knowledge Law
DelveMind never loses its inquiry heritage.

No data family, theory family, experiment family, metric family, procedural family, or failed hypothesis is ever truly deleted.

It may become:
- inactive
- suppressed
- archived
- fossilized
- mythologized
- restricted
- dangerous
- recoverable only through audit

But it is never erased from deep memory.

## 3.7 Skill Preservation Law
Traversal, timing, burden handling, rescue judgment, route planning, witness interpretation, deception reading, custody management, and extraction judgment must remain meaningful.

## 3.8 Readability Law
The world may be deep.
It may not become opaque beyond inhabitability.

## 3.9 Anti-Monoculture Law
No lineage, faction, doctrine, theory, interpretation, or cognitive field regime may permanently erase diversity.

## 3.10 Structured Incompleteness Law
The system must preserve:
- mystery
- silence
- unresolved contradiction
- unknown unknowns
- protected roughness
- constitutional non-totality

Perfect closure is forbidden.
