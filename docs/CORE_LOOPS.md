# Core Loops

## The Traversal & Observation Loop (Micro)
1. **Navigate the Cave:** Enter a procedurally generated cavern. Read the platforming risk, structural scaffolding, and hazard positioning.
2. **Execute & Observe:** Utilize traversal mechanics (coyote time, drops, ropes, bombs) while heavily observing how allies clear obstacles. *Why did someone drop a rope near that hazard? Why did they jump late?*
3. **Loot & Interpret Clues:** Secure physical evidence artifacts. Interpret socially readable environmental clues: footprint paths, dust from falls, bomb blast marks, or faint corruption traces on an artifact. 
4. **Log the Fact:** Update internal suspicion or utilize the lightweight, transparent notebook overlay to quickly tag allies based on these ambiguous physical actions.

## The Encounter Strategy Loop (Run Context)
1. **Clear a Sector:** Push through 2-4 interconnected vertical cavern rooms. The layout, hazards, and loot of this sector are derived deterministically from substreams of the main `run_seed`.
2. **Resource Management:** Spend finite traversal tools to scout safe paths, or to intentionally manipulate cavern routes to split the party and exploit confusion.
3. **Deception & Counterplay:** The Veil executes plausible deniability sabotages disguised within dense action (faking a missed jump, blindly grabbing the "wrong" artifact in chaos). The Warden uses quiet moments to scan evidence. The Scavenger trades information or reroutes artifacts opportunistically.
4. **Secure the Carry:** Enforcing the strict "one-carry" evidence system forces players to juggle items, creating high vulnerability and frantic physical scrambles.

## The Extraction Loop (Run Metagame / Emotional Arc)
1. **Exploration (Lobby & Drop):** Players drop into the monolithic cavern layout generated by the shared seed. Focus is on gathering resources and basic pathfinding.
2. **Suspicion (Descent):** The squad navigates deeper biomes. Ambiguous accidents occur. Artifacts are found to be missing from expected caches. Tension brews.
3. **Chaos (Breakdown of Trust):** Deaths mount. Complex traversal challenges lead to split-second physical errors—both real and intentionally faked. Paranoia escalates as resources dwindle.
4. **Pressure (The Ghost):** The AI "Warden Ghost" entity awakens, relentlessly hunting stragglers and punishing players who try to camp or infinitely delay extraction.
5. **Extraction:** A frantic platforming showdown at the exit rope as players must physically extract while deciding who to trust.
6. **Revelation (Post-Mortem):** Roles are revealed alongside an authoritative timeline of physical events. Surviving players furiously debate the ambiguous facts the timeline presents.

## Meta Loop
- Players develop acute visual literacy for reading environmental traces (lantern light visibility, footprint decays) and hazard timings over repeated runs.
- Social mastery evolves from rudimentary verbal lying into effectively manipulating physics and ambiguous visual clues to construct iron-clad physical alibis.
