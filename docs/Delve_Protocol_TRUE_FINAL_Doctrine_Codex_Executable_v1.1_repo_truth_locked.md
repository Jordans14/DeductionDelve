# 8. Dedicated Combo / Stack Doctrine

## Status lens
- **Live and authoritative:** combo-rich product direction is constitutionally compatible when routed through traversal-first, pressure-first, socially interpreted play; hidden depth is compatible with partial legibility
- **Next-wave target:** formal combo contract, combo visibility rules, anti-dominance governance, replay/explanation compatibility
- **Canonical future:** broader combo classes and more exotic interactions once proof lanes and lifecycle governance are mature

This section exists because a naïve readability doctrine can accidentally cripple one of the project’s strongest futures.

The next wave must preserve and strengthen:
- deep build expression
- weird synergies
- cursed combinations
- delayed realization
- role/item interaction depth
- run-defining combinations

while preventing those systems from becoming arbitrary.

## 8.1 Combo Thesis
Combos are not optional flavor.
They are engines of:
- surprise
- authorship
- replayability
- mastery
- community theorycraft
- expedition identity

## 8.2 Combo Visibility Contract
Every materially meaningful combo must satisfy the following.

### A. Manifestation
The combo must materially manifest through at least one of:
- visual change
- audio cue
- behavioral shift
- route/topology consequence
- burden consequence
- evidence/suspicion consequence
- extraction consequence
- environmental strain or ecology consequence

### B. Provenance
The system must be able to reconstruct:
- source A
- source B
- optional condition C
- resulting effect D

### C. Bounded Scope
The combo must have bounded meaning:
- what it affects
- what it does not affect
- when it is active
- what can interrupt, counter, or constrain it

### D. Explanation Hook
The combo must be compatible with:
- micro explanation
- meso explanation
- post-run explanation
- replay/debug explanation

### E. Runtime Budget Compatibility
The combo must remain compatible with:
- dominant-force curation
- visual governance
- explanation thresholding
- social integrity

## 8.3 Allowed Hidden Depth
The following are explicitly allowed:
- secret synergies
- partial obscurity
- delayed discovery
- cursed upside
- threshold-triggered transformations
- rare role/item crossovers
- continuity-conditioned combos
- odd long-tail combinations

Provided they remain:
- consistent
- inferable
- replay-reconstructable
- non-arbitrary

## 8.4 Forbidden Combo Failure Modes
Not allowed:
- hidden decisive combo outcomes with no lawful signs
- combos that auto-solve social play
- combos that create permanent solved metas
- combos that never materially manifest
- combos whose only effect is silent numeric inflation
- combos that erase the identity of their ingredients

## 8.5 Combo Output Domains
Meaningful combos should cash out into one or more of:

### Topology
routes, blockages, shortcuts, escape paths, containment shapes

### Information
clue reveal, clue distortion, suspicion gradients, confidence shifts

### Social Leverage
plausible deniability, bargaining power, blame pressure, trust stress

### Time Pressure
windows, timers, accelerations, delays, collapse pacing

### Risk Distribution
self-burden, shared burden, delayed cost, redirected pressure

### Environmental State
strain, corruption, visibility, occupancy behavior, recurrence tagging

### Extraction Consequence
carry burden, custody complication, value instability, protocol response

If a combo only changes raw hidden math and never expresses through one of these, it should be re-authored or deprioritized.

## 8.6 Combo Classes for Planning
Codex planning may define and organize combo classes such as:
- amplification
- inversion
- burden transfer
- evidence distortion
- tempo manipulation
- topology control
- extraction destabilization
- recurrence triggering
- cursed power
- camouflage / misdirection
- forced tradeoff

## 8.7 Anti-Dominance Combo Governance
Combo families must be governable through lifecycle patterns:
- cooling
- rarity shift
- altered return
- local suppression
- recontextualization
- successor patterning

## 8.8 Combo Validation
Required validation categories:
- deterministic reproducibility
- manifestation checks
- provenance reconstruction
- dominance scenario coverage
- social integrity coverage
- explanation compatibility coverage

# 9. Multi-Resolution Explanation Doctrine

## Status lens
- **Live and authoritative:** layered truth permissions, public-safe disclosure limits, archive concision, and anti-lore-wall discipline
- **Next-wave target:** unified explanation engine compatible with run truth, public-safe surfaces, and replay
- **Canonical future:** richer cultural/narrative projections that remain faithful to the same underlying event structure

The game cannot hit 9.9 through either:
- constant over-explanation
or
- opaque consequence

The correct target is a multi-resolution explanation architecture.

## 9.1 Explanation Thesis
Explanation is the lawful projection of systemic truth at the right resolution and time.

## 9.2 Micro Layer (0–2 seconds)
Purpose:
- immediate state recognition
- immediate action cue
- flow-preserving reaction

Examples:
- instability spike
- burden rise
- extraction state degradation
- pressure onset
- witness / route alarm

## 9.3 Meso Layer (5–30 seconds)
Purpose:
- cause chain visibility
- player learning
- interpretation adjustment

Examples:
- why instability rose
- why suspicion shifted
- why burden changed
- why a route became unsafe
- why an extraction window worsened

## 9.4 Macro Layer (Post-run / Replay / Audit)
Purpose:
- run reconstruction
- fairness trust
- blame graph
- major event causes
- apex explanation
- recurrence explanation
- what defined the run

## 9.5 Unified Explanation Data Model
All explanation resolutions must derive from the same underlying event/provenance truth.

Forbidden:
- separate handcrafted summary truth
- contradictory debug/player explanations
- post-run narratives that cannot be reconstructed from actual systemic causality

## 9.6 Public-Safe Explanation
Public-safe or player-safe surfaces may conceal protected truth.
They must not lie materially.
They must remain:
- useful
- truthful
- causally grounded
- non-admin-like

## 9.7 Explanation Timing Laws
Not every explanation must appear immediately.
The system must support:
- threshold-based surfacing
- delayed clarification
- post-event reconstruction
- post-run synthesis
- spectator-safe compression

## 9.8 Explanation Anti-Spam Law
Explanation must:
- prioritize dominant causes
- avoid redundant emission
- preserve mystery
- avoid text-wall behavior
- support fast perception first

## 9.9 Explanation Validation
Codex must plan or extend:
- provenance availability checks
- cross-resolution consistency checks
- leakage prevention checks
- major-event explainability scenarios
- replay explanation parity checks

**Forbidden revelations:** hidden role truth, raw ontological admin truth, protected future-state certainty

**Rollback path:** disable new projection layer while preserving source event data

**Truth boundary:** combos may shape lived play but cannot silently replace social interpretation or run authority  
**Inputs:** items, states, environmental tags, continuity tags, pressure conditions  
**Outputs:** manifested effects, provenance chain, explanation-compatible records  
**Movement consequences:** altered route choice, burden handling, evidence interpretation, extraction behavior  
**Forbidden revelations:** invisible decisive forces with no signs  
**Testing plan:** manifestation checks, dominance checks, provenance reconstruction, social integrity checks  
**Cooling plan:** lifecycle suppression / rarity / altered return

## 15.3 Lifecycle Contract
**Owner candidates:** world_memory_service, delvemind_experiment_engine, delvemind_learning_loop, additional family-specific owners  
**Truth boundary:** lifecycle may alter prevalence and meaning, not secretly rig outcomes  
**Inputs:** usage, saturation, heat/cooling, dominance signals, fairness/noise vetoes  
**Outputs:** recurrence states, altered returns, suppression, dormancy, residue  
**Movement consequences:** different route pressures, different carry expectations, different recurrence meaning  
**Testing plan:** saturation scenarios, altered return checks, anti-spam checks  
**Rollback path:** fall back to prior lifecycle weighting while preserving history

## 15.4 Pressure / Phenomenon Contract
**Owner candidates:** DelveKernel → RunGenerator → room/branch/pressure owners  
**Truth boundary:** pressure expresses conditions, not admin omniscience  
**Inputs:** protocol state, branch family, dominant forces/minds, burden state, continuity tags  
**Outputs:** pressure composition, signatures, escalation eligibility, recurrence tags  
**Movement consequences:** route timing, rescue pressure, hesitation, witness interpretation  
**Testing plan:** family distinctness, dominance budget checks, escalation chain checks

## 15.5 Economy / Artifact Contract
**Owner candidates:** item_service, profile/product seams where applicable, generation contract outputs, archive/world-memory seams for aftermath  
**Truth boundary:** economy must remain embodied-play-routed and mechanically neutral where cosmetics are involved  
**Inputs:** artifact class, verification state, burden state, custody state, market/regime state  
**Outputs:** value implications, carry risk, extraction consequence, social leverage shifts, aftermath  
**Movement consequences:** route choice, extraction timing, trust formation, artifact custody behavior  
**Testing plan:** carry-risk scenarios, counterfeit/unstable scenarios, aftermath checks  
**Cooling plan:** rotation/rarity/market response using lifecycle patterns

## 15.6 Social Contract
**Owner candidates:** run/network event timeline owners, evidence surfaces, archive/framing review surfaces  
**Truth boundary:** social play remains contested but lawful  
**Inputs:** player actions, evidence state, route context, burden context, combo/economy crossover where allowed  
**Outputs:** interpretable suspicion pressure, reconstructable blame/evidence, multiple viable readings  
**Movement consequences:** who follows whom, who carries, who hesitates, who is believed
