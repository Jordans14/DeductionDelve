# Deduction Delve — Design Anchor

## The Pitch
Deduction Delve is a cooperative extraction platformer where every jump, trap, and loot decision is evaluated as social evidence. By fusing the physical traversal risk of *Spelunky* with the run variety and item synergies of *The Binding of Isaac*, the game generates the plausible deniability required for true Social Deduction. Players do not solve mysteries in detached voting meetings; they deduce the truth physically by observing movement, survival tactics, item usage, and evidence handling in the wild.

## The Core Gameplay Loop
*   **Exploration:** The team drops into the cavern layout generated by the shared seed, gathering resources and pathfinding.
*   **Suspicion:** Ambiguous accidents occur. Environmental clues (footprints, blast marks) are uncovered, and trust naturally begins to fracture.
*   **Chaos:** Platforming difficulty spikes. Players are separated, bombs are thrown, and saboteurs mask their attacks in the noise of survival.
*   **Pressure:** The AI "Warden Ghost" entity awakens, relentlessly hunting stragglers and punishing players who delay extraction.
*   **Extraction:** A frantic platforming showdown occurs at the exit rope as players must physically extract while deciding who to trust.
*   **Revelation:** Roles are revealed alongside an authoritative timeline of physical events, sparking explosive arguments over what really happened.

## The Five Design Pillars
1. **Social Deduction Through Physical Play:** Truth exists as contestable physical artifacts in a perilous platforming environment, not in UI menus.
2. **Accidents Are Mechanics:** With ropes, bombs, and deadly jumping physics, most kills can be plausibly written off as environmental failures.
3. **Builds Alter Affordances:** The tools players carry (noise dampeners, heavy boots) fundamentally modify the credibility of their physical alibis.
4. **Inference Over Certainty:** Investigation tools support logic and reasoning through probabilistic scores and environmental tells; there is never strict, mechanical hard-proof of guilt.
5. **Readable Chaos:** Deterministic, seeded generation and host-authoritative networking keep the cavern physics perfectly fair and explicitly legible.

## Vertical Slice Scope
The first playable vertical slice must strictly contain only the following:

**Environment**
*   One procedural cavern biome
*   Three room archetypes (Traversal Shafts, Hazard Chokepoints, Evidence Pockets)

**Roles**
*   Warden
*   Veil
*   Scavenger

**Core Tool Sandbox (6–8 Items)**
*   Bomb
*   Rope
*   Lantern Snuffer
*   Heavy Boots
*   Timeline Bookmark
*   Decoy Emitter

**Hazards**
*   Spikes
*   Crush-block traps

**Forensics Layer**
*   Footprints
*   Bomb blast marks
*   Artifact corruption traces

**UX Systems**
*   Suspicion notebook overlay
*   End-of-run timeline replay

## The Six Gameplay Moments We Want Every Match To Produce
1. **The Suspicious Missed Jump:** A player attempts a difficult leap across a spike pit, "misses" the ledge, and plummets. The squad debates if it was a genuine panic or an intentional Veil sabotage disguised as an accident.
2. **The Sabotaged Escape Rope:** A Scavenger discovers that the only rope leading out of a pitch-black cavern has been intentionally placed directly above a previously inactive dart trap.
3. **The Blind Artifact Theft:** A Bomb detonates, filling the evidence room with thick smoke. When the dust settles, the authentic Artifact is gone from the pedestal, and identical sets of footprints lead in three different directions.
4. **The Trap That Fired Too Early:** A deadly crush-block activates half a second before its expected procedural cycle, instantly crushing an ally. Was it an unlucky server seed, or did the Veil use an invisible timing nudge?
5. **The Ghost Run Panic:** The AI "Warden Ghost" pressure mechanic spawns. Surviving players must frantically execute perfect platforming while under intense time pressure, screaming at each other not to block the ladders.
6. **The Post-Mortem Argument:** The run ends via extraction. The timeline reveals "Tick 4022: Artifact Forged." A massive, explosive argument breaks out over voice comms as players desperately trace back their physical paths to figure out who was lingering suspiciously on the scaffolding at that exact timestamp.
